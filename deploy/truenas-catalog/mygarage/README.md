# MyGarage

[MyGarage](https://github.com/jt87/MyGarage-webapp) keeps your tools, manuals, receipts,
consumables and projects in one place, with an insurance inventory and a 3D viewer for STL files.
It works offline on your phone, and everything is stored as Markdown with a history of every change,
on your server or in a GitHub repo you own.

Open it on your home network after installing to create your login and choose where your garage lives.
